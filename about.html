About 1404 Desk

1404 Desk exists so a holder can read both public histories that currently answer to chain ID 1404, keep the raw answers, and leave with a file. It does not exist to win an argument on social media.

How the two histories differ

Public documentation shared by multiple writers describes a common genesis and a common block 316,001, then different block hashes and state roots from block 316,002 at 17 Feb 2026 22:03:01 UTC. This site treats that as a measurement target, not as a courtroom fact it can certify.

Community-family endpoints in config.js include rpc.blockdag.engineering and rpc.welshdag.trade plus additional community operators. Scan-family endpoints include rpc.bdagscan.com and rpc.blockdag.works. Explorers are labelled the same way. None is crowned.

What this cannot do

  • Unfreeze, wrap, claim, or migrate coins.
  • Deliver miners or prove a hardware shipment.
  • Force an exchange listing or a refund of presale cash.
  • Identify the human behind an EOA.
  • Hide an RPC error so one family looks cleaner.

Public reports, by name only

Holders sometimes cite public drafts such as a “Chain 1404 evidence-based community report” and a “Factual response to the technical evidence dossier” (both discussed in August 2026 community threads). This site links no partisan conclusion from those names. Read primary RPC data yourself.

A genesis allocation sweep window of 11 Feb 2026 08:07–08:28 UTC appears in public discussion. It is shown as context text only. This project does not accuse.

Neutrality

If two honest queries disagree, the UI says DIVERGENT. Contributors may pin a checksummed contract address. They may not add a “canonical” badge.
